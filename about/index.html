About

Who I am

Hello! I’m Dominik, a UX/UI designer and lecturer from Warsaw, Poland. I create digital products that balance usability with strong visual impact.

Currently, I design at Flying Bisons and teach at Flying Bisons Academy, while also serving as a Young Jury member at Awwwards. I don’t see limits in design → I love exploring everything from posters to branding, alongside No-Code tools like Webflow and Framer that let me bring ideas to life in exciting new ways.

Services

I design experiences and interfaces for mobile apps and websites, create engaging web designs, and develop branding that brings ideas to life. I also explore the creative potential of AI to craft unique visual concepts and executions.

From the very first conversation, I take time to understand your goals and expectations. My mission is to transform them into digital products and visual identities that not only work seamlessly but also stand out with clarity and aesthetic strength.
