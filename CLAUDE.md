# Dominik Gola — portfolio

Owner: Dominik Gola, UX/product designer. Talks in Polish (short, concrete); site copy is English.
He judges by eye and by how motion *feels*: after any visual change, look at it in the browser before reporting.

## Stack
- Vite 5 multi-page app, vanilla JS (ES modules), no framework. `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173, `npm run build` → `dist/`.
- three.js **0.160.0** (pinned), cannon-es **0.20.0**.
- Pages: `index.html` (Home), `about/index.html`, `drafts/index.html`, `logo/index.html` (internal, not in the nav), `more/index.html` (hidden, key-locked). Add new pages to `pages` in `vite.config.js` (and to the dev redirect regex).

## Drafts (`drafts/`, `src/drafts/`)
**v3 (current): WebGL (three.js) ring of shots seen from INSIDE, white page — `src/drafts/ring.js`.** Built from Dominik's screen recording (Oct 2026):
- Rest: the current shot fills the screen — card ratio = screen ratio inside a 10 px padding (8 on phones), 16 px rounded corners (shader SDF). Title centred right above the picker, counter "01 / 19" bottom left and kind · year bottom right, both as the same small glass pill; they fade with zoom (`--cap`).
- Pulled back, the camera sits in the ring's centre: **every shot is the same size** (`ROW` = 22 % of the height, 30 % on phones — no "current shot is bigger" swap, he found that jumpy) and each card is **wrapped on the ring's cylinder** (vertex shader, `uCurve`), so it reads as being inside the circle; ~4 shots in view. Zoom = the camera gliding from the centre to the shot (interpolated in 1/size so growth is even); the shot flattens as it arrives.
- Arrival: the page opens pulled back on the ring and stays there — the first shot only glides in after the first interaction (`engaged`). Pulled back, corners are `ROW_RADIUS` × rounder.
- Scroll / swipe / arrows / drag: camera eases back (`ZOOM_OUT_K`), the ring turns once it's partly back (`MOVE_AT` 0.6, overlapping), glides in again after `SETTLE_MS` (`ZOOM_IN_K` soft). Clicking a small shot goes there; no zoom-in on click. While moving the middle of each card lags a little (`LAG`, keep subtle).
- **Full-screen mode** (`full`): once a shot has glided in, only the ← → buttons (`.dr__arrow`, nav-pill glass) and the ←/→ keys slide straight to the neighbour at full size (no pull-back, no ring turn). Scroll, swipe, drag and ↑/↓ still pull back to the ring, turn and glide in. Near full screen the shots leave the ring for a flat strip in front of the camera (`b` blend, `L.gapS` leaves a sliver of the next shot in the padding). Click on the shot or Esc → back to the ring, which then stays until the next interaction.
- **Phones**: the ring stands up (vertical, `L.vert`; cards bend along Y), swipe up/down; once a shot is full screen it never goes back to the ring — swipes page through full-screen shots like TikTok (a short flick is enough). No picker, no arrows; title bottom left above the counter.
- Shader note: the rounded-rect AA uses `max(fwidth(d), 1e-4)` — with plain `fwidth` the centre column got `smoothstep(0,0,…)` (undefined) and showed a white seam.
- Content (Oct 2026): his own exports, two separate sets — landscape screens: 19 desktop shots (`public/drafts/d/NN.webp` 2048 px + `NN-lo.webp` 1024 px for the ring; the full-screen shot loads the 2048), phones: 14 portrait shots (`public/drafts/m/NN.webp`, 590 px; no no. 14). Titles/kinds/years (2023–2026) named from what each shot shows, in `DRAFTS` (ring.js). Cover-cropped into the card. No Framer images anymore.
- Picker = the old ruler, drawn white with `mix-blend-mode: difference` so it reads on white and on shots; light progressive blur under it while a shot is full screen.
- Backups: v1 (dark, rounded cards, lightbox) `lab/drafts-v1`, v2 (dark full-height 3:4 wall, CSS 3D) `lab/drafts-v2`.
